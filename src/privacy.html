Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Rockwall Roofing and Gutters, LLC uses the information you provide to respond to roofing inquiries and arrange inspections.

Information you provide

The inspection form collects your name, phone number, property ZIP code, service selection, and any optional message. Please avoid including sensitive information in your message.

How requests are processed

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and routed to rockwallroofingandgutters@gmail.com. FormSubmit processes the submitted details under its own privacy policy. Advertising attribution parameters in the page address, when present, are included with your request to help identify its source.

Website services

Website hosting and Google Fonts may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of serving the website.

Measurement

The page prepares events for phone-link clicks and inspection requests. Those events exclude the contact details and message entered in the form. The page currently does not load a Google Analytics or Google Ads tracking tag. If tracking is enabled later, this policy should be updated to explain the configuration and any applicable choices.

Questions and requests

To ask about your information or request its deletion, email rockwallroofingandgutters@gmail.com or call 972-832-7618.
