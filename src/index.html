What does the free inspection include?

Rockwall Roofing & Gutters offers complimentary roof inspections to assess your roof’s condition and identify potential issues. Tell Johnny what you’ve noticed and ask what the visit will cover for your property, including whether photos or written findings are provided. The inspection scope and appointment are confirmed with the team.

Can you help with an insurance-related roofing project?

Rockwall Roofing & Gutters works with insurance on roofing projects. Speak with Johnny about your situation and confirm what assistance is available, including any coordination with your adjuster or damage documentation. Your insurer determines coverage; the roofing team cannot promise claim approval or payment.

Which roofing materials can I choose from?

Ask Johnny which materials are available for your roof and what he recommends for your property. Before choosing, discuss appearance, product specifications, cost, and maintenance. Any material, impact-resistance rating, or manufacturer certification should be confirmed for the specific product offered.

What warranty will apply to my roof?

Before approving work, ask for the applicable workmanship and product warranty terms in writing. Confirm who provides each warranty, what it covers, its duration, exclusions, and how to request service. The terms for your project should be clear before work begins.

Do you serve Dallas?

Yes. This page is for homeowners in Dallas, Texas. Share your property ZIP code when requesting an inspection so we can confirm the location.

What if I’m not sure I need a new roof?

You can request an inspection to discuss your roof’s condition and options. We provide roof repairs as well as replacements.

What happens after I send the form?

Your request is sent to our team so we can contact you using the phone number you provide. We’ll discuss your concerns and arrange an inspection. Your appointment is confirmed separately.
